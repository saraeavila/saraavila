Research

Advanced Deep Learning for Automated Cochlear Hair Cell Detection

Advisor: Dr. Steven Fernandes, Creighton University · 2024 – 2026

  • Cochlear hair cell quantification is traditionally performed through manual microscopy, limiting the speed and scalability of hearing loss research. This project develops deep learning methods for automated detection and classification of cochlear hair cells.
  • Collected and annotated confocal microscopy images, trained YOLO-based object detection models, and evaluated detection performance against expert annotations to improve automated identification of inner and outer hair cells.

Presented at: FICTA 2025 (Best Paper), NCUR 2025, NeurIPS 2025, NeurIPS 2024

SCRIBE: Transformer-Based Evaluation of Medical School Personal Statements

Advisors: Dr. Steven Fernandes, Dr. Faith Kurtyka · 2025 – 2026

  • Medical school personal statements are often evaluated subjectively, creating inconsistent feedback for applicants. This project develops an offline NLP system that segments essays, classifies rubric-aligned content, and generates structured feedback.
  • Organized and annotated training essays, evaluated transformer embedding models, and refined semantic segmentation and rubric-based classification to improve automated feedback aligned with medical school admissions criteria.

Presented at: ICCV 2025, NeurIPS 2025, Nebraska Academy of Sciences 2025

Enhancing Pharmacy Skills Through AI-Driven Patient Counseling Simulations

Advisors: Dr. Steven Fernandes, Dr. Jessica Cumber, Dr. Kevin Fuji · 2024 – 2025

  • Limited faculty availability restricts pharmacy students' opportunities to practice patient counseling with consistent feedback.
  • Fine-tuned and evaluated LLM options to generate realistic, rubric-aligned patient responses, and designed the simulated patient's profile to support standardized counseling practice.

Presented at: Rush University AI Symposium on Healthcare Education 2025, Nebraska Academy of Sciences 2025
