Volunteer

  • Through the DREAM Scholars program, I mentor children from under-resourced Omaha communities in a service-based after-school setting, providing free after school childcare. I help facilitate STEM activities, educational enrichment, and mentorship while contributing to a supportive environment where students can explore their interests, build confidence, and develop meaningful relationships with college mentors.
  • This has truly been the most fulfilling experience I've had throughout college, working with kids whose experiences remind me of myself and providing them care after school that I wish I had. Working with them and seeing them grow from 2nd to 4th grade has truly been a life altering experience.
