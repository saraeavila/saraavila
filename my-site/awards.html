Next Generation Leaders Program Scholar, 2023 – 2027

  • Provides a full-tuition, four-year scholarship paired with specialized leadership training, coaching, and community-building. It targets high-achieving incoming first-year students who are dedicated to promoting equity, diversity, inclusion, and positive cultural change.

Dean's List, 2023 – 2027

Barry Goldwater Scholar, 2026

  • It is the most prestigious national award for undergraduate students in the U.S. who study science, math, or engineering

Best Paper Award, FICTA 2025

Best Undergraduate Humanities Poster Presentation, Creighton University 2025

Alpha Sigma Nu Jesuit Honor Society, 2025

  • Inducted into Alpha Sigma Nu, the Jesuit Honor Society recognizing students who exemplify scholarship, loyalty, and service. Membership in Alpha Sigma Nu is one of the highest awards a student can receive at a Jesuit institution, such as Creighton University or anywhere within the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities.

S.J. Bucko Scholarship, 2023 – 2024

  • Awarded to a student in Creighton's Student Support Services program who demonstrates outstanding academic achievement, leadership, and financial need. Named in honor of Fr. Raymond A. Bucko, S.J., and supports tuition costs.
